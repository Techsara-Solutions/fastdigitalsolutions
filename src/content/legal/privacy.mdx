## Summary

This website is operated by Fast Digital Solutions LLC ("Fast Digital Solutions", "we", "us"). It is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. We do not use analytics scripts, advertising pixels, marketing cookies, or behavioral tracking. The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to send us through the contact form or by contacting us directly.

## Information we collect

**Contact form submissions.** When you submit the contact form, we receive the information you enter: your first and last name, email address, company, phone number, the topic you want to discuss, and the notes you write about it.

**Direct contact.** If you phone or email us, we receive the information you choose to share.

We do not ask for, and you should not submit, sensitive personal data, passwords, financial account details, government identifiers, or confidential production credentials.

## Why we collect it

We use contact information solely to review your enquiry, respond to you, and assess whether we are a good fit to help. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who receives it

Contact form submissions are delivered to our internal enquiry inbox and are accessible to the team members who handle enquiries. Submissions are transmitted and delivered using cloud infrastructure services acting as processors on our behalf.

## Cookies and tracking

This website does not set marketing or analytics cookies and does not load third-party tracking scripts. Fonts and all other assets are served from this website's own domain.

## Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards, provisionally 12 months for enquiries that do not lead to an engagement, unless a longer period is required for legal or contractual reasons. {/* PENDING: retention period requires owner and legal confirmation before launch (PRD §25.2). */}
