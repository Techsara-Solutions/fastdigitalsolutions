## Our commitment

Fast Digital Solutions LLC wants everyone to be able to use this website, regardless of ability or the technology they use. Accessibility is treated as a design and engineering requirement on this site, not an afterthought.

## Conformance target

This website targets **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines). In practice, that includes:

- Full keyboard operability, including a skip-to-content link and a visible focus indicator
- Semantic structure: landmarks, one descriptive heading per page, and logical heading order
- Form labels, instructions, and error messages that are announced to assistive technology
- Color contrast that meets AA requirements, with state never communicated by color alone
- Support for 200% browser zoom and text reflow without loss of content or function
- Respect for the reduced-motion preference, with no autoplaying or flashing content

## Testing

The site is checked with automated accessibility tooling as part of its build and test pipeline, alongside manual keyboard and screen-reader testing.

## Known limitations

We are not currently aware of accessibility barriers on this website. If we identify limitations, we will list them here along with workarounds while fixes are in progress.
